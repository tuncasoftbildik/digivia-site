Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data DigiVia collects through this website and when you contact us, why we use it, and the rights you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is DigiVia L.L.C-FZ, Meydan Grandstand, 6th floor, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, United Arab Emirates (Meydan Free Zone, licence no. 2649085.01). You can reach us at info@digivia.ae or on WhatsApp at +971 55 486 1805.

2. Scope

This policy covers the website digivia.ae and enquiries you send us. Our products (such as Partner Paneli and Welcome Team) have their own privacy notices, and client projects are governed by the contract and, where applicable, the data processing agreement signed with each client.

3. What we collect

When you contact us by email or WhatsApp: your name, contact details, company and anything you choose to include in your message.

When you visit the site: the site has no contact form, user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking pixels. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

4. Why we use it

To answer your enquiry and prepare proposals (steps prior to a contract), to perform contracts we sign with you, for our legitimate interests in running and securing our business and correspondence, and to meet legal obligations such as accounting and tax record-keeping.

5. Cookies

We do not set cookies or use local storage. Your language is chosen from your browser settings on the first visit and is not stored.

6. Who we share it with

Only service providers we need to operate: email hosting, WhatsApp (Meta), GitHub (website hosting) and Google (fonts), and public authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

7. International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the UAE, for example in the European Union or the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 24 months. Contract and invoicing records are kept for as long as UAE law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you — including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), the EU GDPR and Türkiye’s Law No. 6698 (KVKK) — you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

Write to info@digivia.ae. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Security is our trade. We use encrypted connections, least-privilege access and secret management for the data we hold, and we review access regularly.

11. Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
